Thank you for taking the time to evaluate Quakely!

Your feedback will help us assess the application's software quality and instructional suitability as a supplementary earthquake preparedness learning tool.

WHO CAN PROVIDE FEEDBACK?

The evaluation is intended for selected Grade 4–6 Class Advisers and the SDRMM/DRRM Coordinator of Dr. A. Albert Elementary School.

WHAT WILL BE EVALUATED?

  • Functional Suitability: Does the application provide the intended functions and activities?
  • Performance Efficiency: Does the application perform smoothly and respond appropriately?
  • Interaction Capability: Are the controls, navigation, and interactive elements easy to understand and use?
  • Instructional Suitability: Are the content, activities, and instructions appropriate for the intended educational context?

The first three areas are the selected ISO/IEC 25010:2023 software quality characteristics used in this study.

HOW TO SUBMIT YOUR FEEDBACK

  1. 1. PLAY: Experience the available Quakely activities.
  2. 2. EVALUATE: Consider the application's functionality, performance, interactions, and instructional content.
  3. 3. SUBMIT: Complete the evaluation form below.

About the Evaluation

This evaluation is part of the academic research and system evaluation of Quakely by Team BYTES of National University – Manila.

The study evaluates Functional Suitability, Performance Efficiency, and Interaction Capability based on the ISO/IEC 25010:2023 Product Quality Model, together with the application's instructional suitability.
