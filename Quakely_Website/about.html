TEASER

MISSION & VISION

To provide a localized, child-friendly digital learning experience that complements traditional school earthquake drills. Quakely aims to empower students to act safely and confidently during seismic emergencies by transforming passive lessons into engaging, interactive simulations.

WHY WE MADE IT

The Philippines' position on the Pacific Ring of Fire requires constant readiness. While regular school drills are essential, young learners often need supplementary tools to practice decision-making without feeling scared or overwhelmed. By using a micro-learning framework, stylized Live2D animations, and avoiding graphic disaster media, Quakely creates a supportive psychological buffer where kids can learn critical safety steps at their own pace.

CORE GAMEPLAY

DURING THE QUAKE

Learn how to pack an emergency Go Bag, familiarize yourself with a First Aid kit, and spot dangerous hazards in your living room, kitchen, and classroom.
